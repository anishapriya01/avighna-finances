Cookie Policy

What browser storage and cookies do on this website, and how to control optional use.

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Avighna Techgrow, operating this website and the Avighna Financial Services brand, uses browser storage and may use cookies or similar technologies to operate the site and understand performance. Essential storage is used to remember your cookie choice. Optional analytics or performance technologies are not enabled unless you choose “Accept All”.

1. Essential cookies and storage

Essential technologies support basic site operation, security, and your preferences. Our current consent control stores your selection in your browser’s localStorage as “all” or “essential-only”; this is browser storage, not a traditional cookie. Essential storage is not used to build advertising profiles. Some hosting or security services may use strictly necessary technical storage to deliver or protect the website.

2. Analytics and performance cookies

Analytics or performance technologies, if enabled in the future, may measure aggregated site use, page performance, and technical errors so we can improve the website. They are optional and should remain off unless you select “Accept All”. We do not currently identify or configure a separate analytics provider in the website source, and this policy does not claim that optional analytics are presently active. A future analytics integration will be subject to an updated notice and the applicable consent requirements.

3. Third-party scripts and resources

The website currently requests some presentation resources from external providers, including Google Fonts, the Tailwind CSS CDN, and the Lucide icon library CDN. When your browser fetches a resource from a third party, that provider may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser metadata under its own terms and privacy practices. These resource requests support page presentation; they are not described here as analytics cookies. We do not control third-party services.

4. Your choices and withdrawal

  • Choose Accept All in the banner to record consent for optional analytics/performance technologies, where available.
  • Choose Essential Only to keep optional technologies off, or use the Manage Cookie Preferences button in the footer and then choose Essential Only to withdraw optional consent.
  • You can also disable or clear cookies and local storage through your browser’s privacy settings. Clearing site storage may reset your preference and cause the banner to appear again. Blocking essential storage may affect preference saving or some site functions.

Your selection is stored in this browser on this device. It is not linked to an account. If browser storage is unavailable, the selection can only be applied for the current visit. For privacy questions, email privacy@avighnatechgrow.in or see our Privacy Policy.
