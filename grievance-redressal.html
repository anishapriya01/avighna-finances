Your Data Principal rights

You may request access to information about your personal data, correction or completion, erasure where applicable, withdrawal of consent, or exercise of your right to nominate another person as provided by the DPDP Act, 2023.

How to raise a request

  1. Select the right or grievance type and give the email address or phone number used in your interaction with us.
  2. Describe the request and, if helpful, include the approximate date or service involved. Do not send passwords, account credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information.
  3. Prepare an email addressed to the Grievance Officer using the form below. Review it and send it from your email application. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity before acting.
  4. We will acknowledge and address the request within the period required by applicable law. If you are not satisfied after using this grievance process, you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India as provided by the DPDP Act and applicable rules.
