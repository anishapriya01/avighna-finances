Privacy Policy

How Avighna Techgrow handles personal data when you use this website or contact our advisory team.

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Data Fiduciary: Avighna Techgrow, operating this website and the Avighna Financial Services brand, Pune, Maharashtra, India (“Avighna”, “we”, “us”). This notice describes our intended data practices under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and applicable Indian law. Please contact us if any business identity detail needs correction.

1. Personal data we collect

We seek only information reasonably needed for the purpose for which it is requested. Depending on your interaction, this may include:

  • Contact and inquiry details: name, email address, phone number, service interest, and the business, financial-service, or project details you choose to include in a message.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser and device metadata, request time, and basic diagnostic or security logs generated when your browser connects to the website.
  • Grievance details: information you provide to help us verify and respond to a privacy request or complaint.

Do not send account passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information through a general inquiry form. We do not ask for data that is not needed to respond to your request or provide a service you have asked us to discuss.

2. Purposes and lawful processing

We use personal data for specific, communicated purposes: to answer service inquiries and arrange consultations; to assess, plan, and execute a project or advisory service you request; to communicate about that work; to maintain website security and troubleshoot faults; and to meet applicable Indian tax, corporate, record-keeping, or other legal obligations. We process data on consent or another basis permitted by the DPDP Act, as applicable to the particular purpose. We do not use inquiry details for unrelated promotional messages without a separate choice where required.

3. Data minimisation and sharing

Only information relevant to your request should be submitted. We restrict access to personnel who need it to respond or deliver the requested service. We may use service providers as data processors, including website hosting and infrastructure providers, email delivery providers, and analytics or performance providers if such tools are enabled. These providers are expected to process data only for the services they provide and to use reasonable confidentiality and security safeguards.

We do not currently identify a specific analytics provider in this website’s source. We will not enable optional analytics or performance cookies before the visitor has made a choice where consent is required. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of the website, our users, or our business.

4. Security and transfers

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and our operations, which may include SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit, restricted account access, and secure handling by relevant service providers. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Where a processor or infrastructure provider handles information outside India, we will do so subject to applicable Indian law and any restrictions notified under the DPDP Act.

5. Retention and erasure

We retain personal data only for as long as it is needed for the stated purpose, to address an active request or engagement, or to comply with applicable Indian tax, corporate, or other legal retention duties. Once the purpose is fulfilled, or when valid consent is withdrawn and no other legal basis or retention requirement applies, we will erase the data or cause it to be erased within a reasonable period. Limited records may be retained where the law requires it or for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

6. Your rights as a Data Principal

Subject to the DPDP Act and applicable rules, you may ask us to:

  • provide access to information about the personal data being processed and a summary of its processing;
  • correct or complete inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • erase personal data when it is no longer needed or when the applicable conditions for erasure are met;
  • withdraw consent for processing based on consent, without affecting processing that took place before withdrawal or processing permitted on another lawful basis; and
  • exercise your right to nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, as provided by law.

To make a request, email privacy@avighnatechgrow.in with the right you wish to exercise and enough information for us to identify the relevant interaction. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity and protect against an unauthorised disclosure. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. You may also use the Grievance Redressal portal.

7. Cookies and third-party resources

Our site uses local browser storage to remember your cookie choice. The site also loads certain presentation resources from third parties, such as font and interface libraries. Those providers may receive technical connection data when your browser requests those resources. Read our Cookie Policy for controls and details about optional cookies.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this notice to reflect changes in our services, technology, or applicable law. The effective and updated dates above will change when the notice is revised. For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@avighnatechgrow.in or see our Grievance Redressal page.
